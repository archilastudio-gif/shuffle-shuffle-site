Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Shuffle Shuffle ("the app", "we") is a relaxing puzzle game. This policy explains how data is handled when you use the app.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress, coins, streaks, certificates, chosen nickname, and language preference are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not operate an account or game-data server. Uninstalling the app deletes this local data.

Optional rewarded ads

The app uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded ads. You decide whether to watch an ad for an in-game benefit, such as an extra hint, a same-level revive, or doubled Daily coins. Rewarded ads do not interrupt normal play.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google: your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostic information such as crash and performance data, and device or other identifiers including the advertising ID or app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. Data is encrypted in transit.

Consent and choices

Where required, the app asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads and provides a Privacy choices option in Settings when available. You can decline where offered; the game remains playable without watching rewarded ads. You can also manage or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children's privacy

The app's Google Play target audience is ages 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13. Ad content is limited to a G rating.

Third-party service

Ad serving is provided by Google AdMob and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices and contact

You may delete locally stored game data by uninstalling the app. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to archila.studio@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this policy and its date when our data practices change.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 17 日

《Shuffle Shuffle》（“本应用”“我们”）是一款轻松的益智游戏。本政策说明你使用本应用时数据如何被处理。

保存在设备上的游戏数据

你的进度、金币、连胜、证书、昵称和语言偏好只保存在设备的应用私有空间。我们不运营用户账号或游戏数据服务器。卸载应用会删除这些本地数据。

自愿观看的奖励广告

本应用使用 Google AdMob 提供可选的奖励广告。你可以自行决定是否观看广告，以获得额外提示、原关卡复活或每日金币翻倍等奖励。奖励广告不会主动打断正常游戏。

Google 移动广告 SDK 可能自动收集并与 Google 分享：IP 地址（可用于估算大致位置）、应用互动（如启动、点击和视频观看）、诊断信息（如崩溃与性能数据），以及设备或其他标识符（包括广告 ID 或应用集 ID）。Google 将这些信息用于广告、分析，以及防欺诈、安全和合规。数据在传输过程中会加密。

同意与选择

在法律要求的地区，本应用会在请求广告前通过 Google 用户消息平台征求同意，并在可用时于设置中提供“隐私选择”。你可以在提供选项时拒绝；不观看奖励广告也可以继续游玩。你也可以在 Android 设置中管理或重置广告 ID。

儿童隐私

本应用在 Google Play 的目标受众为 13 岁及以上，并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。广告内容分级上限设置为 G。

第三方服务

广告由 Google AdMob 提供，并受 Google 隐私政策约束。我们不会出售个人数据。

你的选择与联系方式

卸载本应用即可删除本地游戏数据。如有问题或隐私请求，请发送邮件至 archila.studio@gmail.com。

政策变更

数据做法发生变化时，我们会更新本政策及日期。
