Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Shuffle Shuffle ("the app", "we") is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the app uses today, and what it will use as optional features are added in future updates.

The current version: we collect nothing

The version available today collects, transmits, and shares no personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, and no user accounts. We have no servers.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — levels, coins, streaks, certificates, your chosen nickname, and language preference — is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves your phone, and uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Features planned for future updates

As the game grows, some optional features will be added that involve data. Each is described here in advance so this policy stays stable:

  • Usage analytics (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase): to understand how the game is played — such as which levels are too hard — so we can improve it. This collects app-usage and device information, not your identity.
  • Opt-in rewarded ads (Google AdMob): you may choose to watch an ad for an in-game reward (for example, to double a bonus). Ads are always your choice and never interrupt play. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to serve and measure ads.
  • Optional online features (e.g., leaderboards or cloud save via Google Play services): these require only the basic account information you choose to connect.

Before any of these features goes live, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play "Data safety" information so they always match what the app actually does.

Children's privacy

The app is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. The current version collects no data from anyone. When ads or analytics are added, they will be configured appropriately for a general audience.

Third-party services

When the optional features above are enabled, they are provided by Google (Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play services) and governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy whenever our data practices change and revise the "Last updated" date above.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 6 月 13 日

《Shuffle Shuffle》（"本应用""我们"）从设计之初就尊重你的隐私。本政策说明本应用当前会使用哪些数据，以及未来版本加入可选功能后会使用哪些数据。

当前版本：我们什么都不收集

目前提供的版本不收集、传输或分享任何个人数据。没有广告、没有统计分析、没有用户账号，我们也没有服务器。

保存在你设备上的数据

你的游戏进度——关卡、金币、连胜、证书、你设定的昵称和语言偏好——只保存在你自己的设备上，存放于应用私有空间，永远不会离开你的手机；卸载应用即永久删除。

未来版本计划加入的功能

随着游戏成长，会加入一些可选的、涉及数据的功能。在此提前说明，让本政策保持稳定：

  • 使用统计（如 Google Analytics for Firebase）：用于了解游戏被如何游玩——例如哪些关卡太难——以便改进。它收集应用使用情况和设备信息，不涉及你的身份。
  • 自愿观看的奖励广告（Google AdMob）：你可以选择观看广告以换取游戏内奖励（例如让奖励翻倍）。广告永远由你自愿触发，绝不打断游戏。AdMob 可能使用你设备的广告标识符来投放和衡量广告。
  • 可选的联网功能（如通过 Google Play 服务的排行榜或云存档）：仅需要你主动连接的基本账号信息。

在上述任何功能上线之前，我们都会更新本政策以及应用在 Google Play 的"数据安全"信息，确保它们始终与应用的实际行为一致。

儿童隐私

本应用适合全年龄段、老少咸宜。当前版本不向任何人收集数据。日后加入广告或统计时，会按面向大众受众的方式妥善配置。

第三方服务

当上述可选功能启用时，它们由 Google 提供（Google AdMob、Google Analytics for Firebase、Google Play 服务），受 Google 隐私政策约束。

政策变更

每当我们的数据做法发生变化，我们都会更新本政策并修改上方的"最后更新"日期。

在上述任何功能上線之前，我們都會更新本政策以及應用在 Google Play 的「資料安全」資訊，確保它們始終與應用的實際行為一致。
